Champion: **smr_modern** (5–0).

## Decisive reasons
### physiology
After amendment both finalists use the same purity toolkit: location-free labels, gaze-contingent fixation, held-out mappings and pixel replay. The difference is the physiology and the deliverable.

**smr_modern (B).** It rests on the strongest scalp signal ds004362 offers. Feet vs hands sustains to 1–2 s at 0.667 per 1-s window. I recomputed this from sf2_smr/res.json: 0.554/0.646/0.667/0.629/0.610. That converts to a dry y of r ≈ 0.25–0.33 per 1-s window, which can drive continuous velocity. It runs over tens of hours with feedback, the regime in which SMR control is documented to improve (Wolpaw 2004, Perdikis 2018). It honestly downgrades x to "earned". The 33% of subjects above floor at 2–3 s that it cites reproduces exactly. Its shadow-decoder positive control is the one element in either design that proves the leak tests can detect a leak: it trains a deliberately display-leaky decoder on the same person and data, and that decoder must fail where the deployed one passes. That is the RLNF lesson turned into a figure. Its decomposition of the PI's hypothesis is signed and falsifiable, and it is consistent with E2b and E6: H1 predicts decoder volume stays flat, H2 that robustness rises, H3 that the user learns.

**population (A).** Its physiology deliverable is a single-session 4-class selector at 0.02–0.045 bits/s, with about 1 s of lag. By its own admission "continuous" is nominal and assistance would dominate. That is below the established floor. Its headline scaling increment has already gone the wrong way on real data: on L/R, the EEG − E2 slope is −.0075 [−.0141, −.0015], which I verified in pop_final/central11.json. Its rescue depends on a prospective G2 pilot showing that surrogates fall to floor on its new labels, which is a hope.

**Where A is ahead.** It is better on rigour of the scaling measurement: a people × windows-per-person surface, a noise-decomposition power analysis, and margin-based criteria. It is also more feasible.

**Net.** B's real continuous-control signal, positive-control purity and bitter-lesson decomposition outweigh A's advantages on the weighted rubric.

Graft from the loser: smr_modern should adopt population's power-gated scaling measurement in place of its weakly powered pooled ladder.

1. **Fit a scaling surface on location-free labels.** Measure the people axis × windows per person (N × k) on L1/L2b labels, pooling the broad cohort with the Stieger prior. This separates "saturates in people" from "saturates because each person gave few trials".
2. **Estimate power from data.** Take a noise decomposition like pop_final/noise.json, where 43–73% of the per-subject gain variance is trial-sampling noise at 45 trials. Use it to project the SE of the H1, H2 and H3 slopes.
3. **Pre-register an expansion rule.** If the month-2 dry pilot projects power below 80%, enlarge the held-out test set (or the number of fixed held-out sessions) before collection.
4. **Run a G2-style go/no-go gate.** In the month-2/3 dry pilot, require that the upper 95% CI of the surrogate-union share of the EEG margin is below 25% on each axis separately, with a declared pivot if it fails.

### scaling
After amendment the two finalists share most of their purity machinery: central-11 montage, held-out rot90/rot270 mapping β tests, frame-exact pixel replay, gaze-contingent fixation, location-free body-part labels, and the S7 posterior-alpha surrogate. They therefore differ on two things: what each delivers, and how it shows the scaling answer. Population scores higher on the tournament question, "is there an evidenced mechanism by which more data buys control, and where does it saturate". Its N × k-per-person × own-hours surface is the right ML experiment for separating "saturates in people" from "saturated at 45 trials per person" (E2's blind spot). Its slopes are file-backed (G1 CIs exclude 0) and its power analysis is grounded in data (noise.json). Its weakness is that its own increment is underpowered at the low end, which it concedes, and the expected answer is about 1 point per doubling. smr_modern answers the same question with a sharper, signed, mechanistic decomposition: H1 decoder volume flat, consistent with E2b +0.005, E6 and the simulator; H2 robustness rises through dry-cap non-stationarity; H3 operant user learning. It uses recency- and practice-controlled designs and has separate falsifiers. This is nearly as good on scaling and more honest about where one person's data saturates. The win comes from the heavily weighted physiology and purity criteria. smr_modern delivers continuous, Kalman-filtered velocity control from 1-s windows: y primary, x earned per person with a pre-registered F5 fallback, and per-window r figures I checked against its own d′ conversion. Population delivers a 2-s, roughly 1-s-lag, 4-direction selector at 0.02–0.045 bits/s and concedes that "continuous is nominal". smr_modern also adds the one purity element population lacks, a sighted-label shadow decoder as a leak positive control. That shows the P2/P3 tests can actually detect a screen-following decoder, which turns "β_screen ≈ 0" from an absence of evidence into a calibrated negative. Both proposals use instructed imagery, so naturalness is close. smr_modern withdraws instruction for use and certifies blind "just intend" at ≥ 70%, while population's hint-free block is expected at chance.

Graft from the loser: smr_modern should adopt population's N × k scaling surface and its data-derived power analysis. On its 20 broad training participants plus 4 deep participants, it should fit the pooled dry ladder as a grid over people (2–20) × labelled windows per person (45/90/180/full), for both Model A and Model B. Like population's surface, the grid separates "saturates in people" from "saturated because each person gave 45 trials", which E2 cannot do. That would give smr_modern's conceded people axis an exchange rate against own hours instead of leaving it to E2's gel numbers. It should also borrow population's noise decomposition (the trial-sampling vs heterogeneity share of the per-subject slope variance) to set the number of test windows and test participants needed for H1's "≤ +0.03 r" and H2/H3's "≥ +0.05" claims to be powered. Population's per-quintile zero-shot practice curve is a cheap second graft for measuring the instruction burden in session 1.

### validity
After both amendments the two designs share most of their purity machinery: central-11 SMR, body-part labels, gaze-contingent fixation, held-out rot90/rot270 leak signature and pixel replay. They differ in three places.

First, smr_modern delivers what the brief asks for: continuous closed-loop velocity control, certified in closed loop. Its decoder trains only on fixation probes and on blind, label-symmetric closed loop. Its primary purity figure has a sensitivity check that no other design in the bracket has: the sighted-ReFIT shadow decoder, built on the same person and data, must show β_screen > 0 in the same blocks, or the test is declared insensitive. That turns the RLNF trap into a measured contrast instead of an assumed absence.

Second, population's deliverable is a 4-direction selector at 0.02–0.045 bits/s whose own author calls it nominally continuous, tested in one 88-min session with 14 minutes of closed loop. Its headline people-axis slope is admittedly underpowered at the low end. On ds004362 its EEG−eye-proxy slope is significantly negative on L/R (−.0075 [−.0141, −.0015]), and E2 already shows that axis saturating at 32–48 people.

Third, smr_modern meets the PI's bitter-lesson hypothesis head-on, for one person sitting for tens of hours. It makes three signed, separately falsifiable predictions on fixed held-out sessions with random-k volume control: H1, decoder volume is flat; H2, robustness rises; H3, user skill rises. That beats population's 4×40 h depth arm, which is secondary to its cohort.

smr_modern is also honest about a 1-D y fallback (F5). Its physiology is weak on x but grounded in its own time-resolved ds004362 analysis. Population edges ahead only on scaling breadth (the N×k surface) and on simpler sessions, which is not enough to offset purity, physiology and generality.

Graft from the loser: smr_modern should take two things from population.

1. Restrict display-cued public data to unlabelled use. smr_modern currently trains its day-1 population prior on Stieger 2021 and ds004362 labels, whose targets were drawn on screen. That contradicts its own rule that no decoder label is ever computed from anything visible. Population's fix: use public display-cued data only for self-supervised pretraining and rest re-centring. The supervised prior then comes from smr_modern's own fixation-probe (L1) and blind closed-loop (L2b) data, pooled across its 32 dry participants.

2. Adopt population's clean-trial co-primary and power plan:
   - The clean-trial and microsaccade-incongruent tests are stated on the above-chance margin: margin ratio ≥ 0.8, lower CI ≥ 0.6.
   - A noise decomposition (trial-sampling vs heterogeneity) sets test-window counts in advance.
   - A pre-registered rule expands the held-out test set if the month-2 dry pilot projects power below 80%.

   smr_modern needs this because its pooled P2 test and its H2/H3 slopes rest on near-floor margins.

Optionally, fit population's 2-D scaling surface on smr_modern's pooled data: people (N) × labelled windows per training person (k). That would give the people-vs-own-hours exchange rate smr_modern currently concedes it cannot measure.

### editor
Both finalists concede the paradigm and the rate are not new and both name Nature Communications as the venue, so the question is which headline survives review and matters beyond BCI. smr_modern has a concrete, reviewable, broadly interesting figure. A decoder trained only on labels nothing visible can identify follows body intent under held-out rotations and pixel replay. A shadow decoder trained on the same person's sighted ReFIT labels follows the screen. That is a positive-controlled demonstration of shortcut learning in closed-loop neural decoding: it quantifies the RLNF trap and then closes it. It also pre-registers a signed, falsifiable decomposition of the PI's bitter-lesson hypothesis: decoder volume stays flat (consistent with E2b and E6), while robustness and user skill rise. Its label rule is now absolute: every decoder label comes from L1 fixation probes or blind L2b with a symmetric tone. Its y axis (feet vs hands) is the better-supported signal. I reproduced from res.json the gel per-1-s value of 0.667 (r≈0.40) and the full-window value of 0.700. It also delivers continuous 1-D control, and 2-D where x is earned, at 0.03–0.3 bits/s. population is now carefully audited, but its headline is a measurement whose expected answer is about 1 point per doubling, on a selector of 0.02–0.045 bits/s. On the only real data, the eye proxy out-scales EEG on L/R (−.0075 [−.0141, −.0015], verified in central11.json), and its powered dry slope is conceded to be marginal at the low end. It also keeps {identity, rot180} for closed-loop co-adaptation updates with visible rings. That is the same active-axis leak it successfully attacked in smr_modern; it is bounded by the rot90/270 tests but still present in its co-adaptive and depth-arm training data. Reviewers would demand of smr_modern: (1) more than 4 deep participants behind H1/H3, or pooled broad-cohort estimates; (2) evidence that the shadow-vs-deployed difference comes from the label and not from the different training blocks (S vs L1/L2b), for example a shadow trained on L2b-matched amounts of S data; (3) the fraction of x earners and a 1-D fallback that is pre-registered as the primary claim; (4) evidence that blind auditory-feedback learning does not stall. These are answerable. population's core result (a near-flat slope on a near-floor selector) is not something an editor would send out with enthusiasm.

Graft from the loser: smr_modern should take population's data-driven power machinery and its people × windows-per-person grid, and apply them to its own location-free labels. First, run population's noise decomposition: split the per-subject variance into trial-sampling and heterogeneity parts (noise.json shows sampling is 43–73% at 45 trials). Use it to size the broad cohort and the number of held-out test participants. Adopt the pre-registered rule that expands the test set (up to 48) if a month-2/3 dry pilot projects power below 80%. Second, add population's prospective G2 go/no-go gate (12 pilots; upper CI of the surrogate-union share of the margin < 25% on each axis) before the deep arm starts. Third, fit population's N × k accuracy surface (people × L1/L2b windows per person, linear vs deep) on the broad cohort plus the Stieger/ds004362 prior. This turns smr_modern's conceded people axis into the 'exchange rate between people and own hours'. That completes its bitter-lesson decomposition: people vs own volume vs robustness vs user skill.

### lab
Asked as the lab manager would ask it: smr_modern is the design this lab can finish, and it gives the PI the better first-of-its-kind figure. It fits the stated resources: 4 long recorders at about 60 h each, plus 28 broad participants, which is "a cohort of tens", on 2 rigs with 2 RAs. Population needs about 144 people with a full eye-tracker, chin-rest, 12-EMG and IMU setup in a 6-month window, plus a 3rd RA. smr_modern also delivers a real continuous effector. Feet versus hands on the y axis, at a dry r of about 0.25–0.33 per 1-s window, is well supported by its own ds004362 time course, and x is earned per person with a pre-declared 1-D fallback. Population ends as a 0.02–0.045 bits/s 4-direction selector whose cursor motion is mostly assistance. On purity, smr_modern is stronger. No decoder label is ever computed from anything visible, and the closed-loop held-out-mapping test (C2c) and pixel replay are judged against a sighted-label shadow decoder that serves as a positive control. That gives the one figure in the tournament that quantifies and closes the RLNF trap. Its H1/H2/H3 split meets the PI's "sit, collect, retrain, adapt" directly, as signed, separately falsifiable predictions on fixed held-out sessions. Population's N×k scaling surface is the better scaling design, but its central increment claim is already weakened on real data: on L/R the eyes significantly out-scale EEG (−.0075 [−.0141, −.0015]). Its people axis is known to saturate on gel (E2), and its own low-end power is conceded as underpowered. Population's gains are in the scaling and purity rows only. They do not make up for its large deficits in physiology, feasibility and generality.

Graft from the loser: smr_modern should take two things from population. First, the prospective G2 go/no-go pilot gate with its power rule. In month 2–3, before the deep and broad cohorts start, run about 10–12 dry pilots through the full session. Require the upper 95% CI of the auditor/surrogate-union share of the EEG margin to be below 25% on y, x and 4-class separately. Use the measured dry penalty and the per-subject noise decomposition to size the fixed held-out test set: expand it beyond 8 people if projected power is below 80%. Pre-declare what shrinks (the broad cohort) and what grows (the deep arm) if the gate fails. Second, use the broad plus deep dry data together with Stieger 2021 to fit population's people × windows-per-person (N×k) grid. Run it for the linear model and for EEGNet/transformer sizes, on fixed test people. The PI's bitter lesson then gets at least a matched exchange-rate estimate between "more people" and "more own hours", rather than a conceded people axis.

## Completeness critic (all gaps judged valid at least in part; revision log in the design of record)

- [critical] §7 decoder table vs INTERFACES.md data_engine: The data engine cannot train the deployed decoder, yet the design marks it 'implemented' for that role. BodyCoordinateDecoder.fit raises unless every window carries provenance source L1/L2b, but data_engine provenance has only {block, feedback_visible, session, t, trial, display_goal}, with no `source`. Trainer.fit returns a WeightedVelocityDecoder (bandpower ridge), not the Riemannian central-11 model. Labeler's label is the screen-geometry unit vector from cursor to revealed goal, not the cued body vector. ChampionChallenger scores 2-D mean cosine, which does not fit per-axis logistic outputs with x=0 when x is unearned.
- [critical] §7 / §4 label rule vs protocol.py: The design wires the deployed decoder as Trainer(blocks=("cued_blank","blank_feedback")). In the implemented protocol, blank_feedback uses cue 'target', so the goal is drawn at its screen position and only the cursor is hidden. Its labels are therefore display-identifiable, which breaks the design's own rule. The L2b block (hidden goal, cue as in L1) does not exist in the code. INTERFACES also requires blank_feedback to come 'straight after a closed_loop block', but the design's session order puts L2b (30–50 min) before S (50–65 min).
- [critical] §5 certification on blank-cue windows: The verdict of record cannot fail on most of its checks. On L1/L2b windows the display surrogate is zero by construction, so DISPLAY_LEAK cannot fire. FEEDBACK_CIRCULAR needs closed_loop vs blank_feedback windows, which are excluded. With gaze-contingent fixation the eye-tracker auditor is constant, and purity.py marks it 'skipped: constant on certifying windows (fixation held)'. EVIDENCE.md's own simulation says the same: with gaze leak in every person, 'the verdict stays PASS (the certifying windows are clean by design)'.
- [critical] §5 P2 held-out mappings with 1-D y: For the most likely headline (1-D y, with x unearned in about 80% of people), rot ±90 maps body-y to screen-x. A reachable 1-D task puts targets left or right, so the screen regressor on body-y (screen-vertical offset) is 0 on every trial and β_screen cannot be estimated. Mirror-x leaves y collinear, which certify returns as TEST_INSENSITIVE. The design never specifies H-block target geometry, so the primary endpoint may be untestable.
- [critical] §9 power for P2: The P2 power calculation delivers about 50% power, not 'powered'. Requiring the upper CI of β_screen < 10% at a true 0 with SE = 0.05 gives P(β̂ + 1.96·SE < 0.10) = Φ(0.04) ≈ 0.52. 80% power needs SE ≤ 0.036, i.e. about 4,900 (ρ 0.4) to 8,700 (ρ 0.3) identifiable trials. ρ_trial 0.3–0.4 has no derivation and exceeds the design's own dry per-window r of 0.10–0.25. The supply of '9,000 = 30 H blocks × 50 × 6' is overcounted: H starts at session 3, C2b and C2c alternate, 15% of C2c are replays, and mirror-x trials are unusable for y. Broad '~3,200' does not match H in sessions 3–5 (up to 4,800).
- [high] §5/§9 certify margin and F1: The 'trained margin' is never defined. When no margin is supplied, certify.py estimates it as β_body + β_screen and says so itself: bounding β_screen < 10% 'already puts β_body above 90%'. With that estimate the 70% β_body criterion and F1's 'β_body lower CI < 50%' are nearly tautological: a decoder with tiny, noisy outputs still scores about 100% relative to its own margin.
- [high] §9 pooling vs certify implementation: The design says pooled y uses 'a mixed model'. certify.py implements a trial-cluster (session, trial) bootstrap OLS, which ignores person-level clustering. Pooling 6 deep people (about 9,000 trials) with 32 broad people (about 3,200) lets 6 people dominate the pooled verdict and makes CIs anti-conservative.
- [high] §4/§7 preprocessing: CAR leak into central-11: RealTimeLoop applies CAR over all good channels, and config.gate_exempt keeps FP1/FP2 in even during blinks. Frontal/ocular potentials, saccadic spike potentials (Yuval-Greenberg 2008) and temporalis/frontalis EMG from F7/F8/T7/T8 are therefore subtracted into every central-11 channel at about 1/n. 'Central-11 excludes frontal channels by construction' is false after CAR. The body_decoder docstring also admits that the CP channels share posterior sources.
- [high] §5 PASS rule vs purity.py: The design says occipital and every auditor surrogate 'must sit at its floor'. The implemented PASS flags only OCULAR_LEAK (frontal/slow beating its floor at Bonferroni α/2 AND reaching ≥ 50% of full skill), DISPLAY_LEAK and FEEDBACK_CIRCULAR. Occipital is 'reported; not flagged'. A frontal surrogate at 49% of full skill passes. The certify-window path's GAZE_TRACKS_LABEL / NO_CORTICAL_EVIDENCE verdicts are not documented in INTERFACES.md.
- [high] §5 purity audits the wrong model: PurityAudit's 'full' decoder and surrogates are a ridge on bandpower features (VelocityDecoder, feature_set='bandpower', all 32 channels). The deployed decoder is the central-11 Riemannian tangent-space logistic model. The surrogate-to-full ratios (for example OCULAR_FRACTION) therefore say nothing directly about the deployed decoder.
- [high] §3/§5 eye proxy on L/R (unreported level): The cited file pop_final/central11.json shows the 1–8 Hz eye proxy (feature set 'E2', which includes VEOG/HEOG) decoding imagined L/R cross-subject at 0.72 → 0.766 (8 → 76 people), above central-11 8–30 Hz at 0.663 → 0.681. The design reports only the slope difference (−.0075). The 'responders keep L/R 0.745/0.709/0.671' claim of sustained lateralised SMR is therefore not separable from eye signals. For feet/fists, EEG beats the eye proxy (0.684 vs 0.607), which supports y as primary.
- [high] §7 adaptation vs frozen measurements: Re-centring (τ = 5 min) runs 'on all blocks'. Supervised updates come every 3 min during L2b, plus nightly retrains and promotions. Yet 'test blocks frozen' and H3 needs 'frozen-decoder blind L2b online accuracy'. It is unclear which L2b blocks are frozen and whether re-centring runs during H/R test blocks. Re-centring over class-imbalanced stretches (runs of one cue, 1-D H blocks) also removes part of the class mean.
- [high] §9 H1–H3 power and falsifiers: There is no power analysis for H1 (TOST ±0.03 r), H2 (+0.05 r) or H3 (+0.05 accuracy) with n = 6. At L1 n = 120 per session the binomial SE is about 0.045, so a +0.05 per-person change is about 1 SE and '≥ 4 of 6' is underpowered. F3b/F3c ('CI includes 0') treat absence of evidence as falsification and will fire from underpowering alone. H1 gets an equivalence test, H2/H3 do not.
- [high] PI hypothesis / scaling regime: The design cannot reach the regime where the evidence says scaling pays. Per person it caps at 50 h of cap time (100 h for 2), of which about 35/90 min is labelled and L1 uses 3 s of each ~7.5-s probe: roughly 12–19 h of labelled EEG, comparable to Stieger per person. The brief allowed hundreds of hours. H4 tops out at N = 20–26 training people, below E2's linear saturation (32–48) and the EEGNet crossover (≥ 64), and public display-cued data are banned from supervision. The answer to 'people vs hours' and to the PI's bitter lesson is close to predetermined.
- [high] §9 G2 gate: The G2 gate cannot be evaluated as written. Pilots have 2 sessions, but H blocks start at session 3, so neither 'shadow sensitivity demonstrated' nor 'a dry penalty that leaves P2 powered' has any H/R data behind it. Comparing dry vs gelled over 2 sessions confounds electrode type with session order unless counterbalanced, which is not stated. The '4-class' criterion is a leftover from the population design, with no 4-class decoder here.
- [medium] §5 P2 confound: distribution shift and remapping: The deployed decoder trains only on fixation-only, scene-free windows and is tested in H blocks with moving eyes, a visible moving cursor and the cognitive load of a rotated mapping. A low β_body can come from covariate shift or remapping difficulty, but F1 labels it 'not cortex'.
- [medium] §5 P3 pixel replay: A cursor seen moving toward A can change the person's actual intent (they follow it) or evoke action-observation and spatial-attention mu modulation over central sites, which is cortical, not circular. β_replay > 0 and 'passive watching above floor' would then trigger F2 for the wrong reason. The design also does not say how goal B is 'pre-committed' without displaying it, which would make B display-identifiable.
- [medium] §8 expected performance: The '1-D hit rate at 10 s 0.62–0.79' assumes 10 independent 1-s windows at constant r. ds004362 trials are 4 s long and the design's own table shows decay (feet/fists 0.667 → 0.610 at 3–4 s; L/R 0.537), and overlapping windows with slow drift are not independent. Time-to-target, required by brief item 4, is missing. No literature anchor in bits/s or success is used (Edelman 2019, McFarland 2010). The 1-D throughput of 0.01–0.05 bits/s partly falls below the 0.03–0.3 band, contrary to §1's 'bottom of the band'.
- [medium] §4 L2b labels: Every window of a ~12-s blind trial gets the cued body vector, although ERD is transient, decaying after about 2 s, and the person may stop imagining once the progress tone peaks. The windowing rule (settle time, exclusion after the goal is reached) is unspecified. The 3-min supervised updates are trained on this noisy label.
- [medium] §7 numbers vs code: '62.5-ms hop via loop_config' does not hold: loop_config only sets window_s, and config.task.control_hz = 10 Hz (100 ms). The 'EEG 30–90 Hz rim-power veto' cannot be computed in the implemented chain: CausalFilter low-passes at 40 Hz with a 50-Hz notch, and Preprocessing.decimate_to_hz = 100. sim_body is listed 'to build' but neural_cursor/sim_body.py exists. 'Auditors reach purity.py and certify.py' is wrong, because certify.py states it sees only decoder outputs and geometry.
- [medium] Idle gate / safety: The §4 label table has no rest or idle label source. A 2p−1 logistic output is never 0 at rest, so the cursor and robot drift. ds004362 idle-vs-imagine (0.844) is display-confounded. 'False commands ≤ 1/min' is pre-registered with no training data and no measurement block, yet the real arm (from session 20) depends on it, as do grasp and z-switch 'dwell actions' that are undefined for a 1-D decoder.
- [medium] §1 naturalness / F4: The design is instructed feet-vs-hands MI with an arbitrary feet=up mapping. The brief demands an honest argument for instructed MI, and none is given. F4 cannot distinguish natural intention from habitual MI: 'just intend' is scored by a decoder trained only on cued feet/hands imagery, so success means the person kept doing feet/hands MI. How the goal is conveyed in blind 'just intend' is unspecified: a cue makes it cued, a location makes it display-identifiable.
- [medium] §9 multiplicity: F1 fires on 'any of' about 10 purity tests (β_body, β_screen, P1, blank-cue flags, S7, clean-trial, microsaccade, cue-epoch, cross-modal, label audit, localiser), each at 5%, with no family-wise control. A spurious failure is likely (about 40–50% for 10–15 independent tests). Pass criteria also use per-test CI bounds.
